Deferred Interest Promotions — IMPORTANT

From time to time, Premier Choice Bank may offer special deferred interest promotions on qualifying purchases (e.g., "No interest if paid in full within 12 months"). These offers differ from our standard 0% APR introductory offer in a critical way:

DEFERRED INTEREST WARNING: Under a deferred interest promotion, if you do not pay the entire promotional balance in full before the promotion expires, you will be charged interest on the original purchase amount dating back to the date of purchase — not just the remaining balance. At 29.99% APR, a $1,500 purchase not fully paid after 12 months results in approximately $449.85 in retroactive interest charges added to your balance in a single billing cycle.

Deferred interest promotions are separate from the standard 18-month 0% introductory APR. Minimum payments during a deferred interest promotion are calculated on your total account balance and may not be sufficient to pay off the promotional balance before the promotion expires. We do not guarantee that minimum payments will retire the promotional balance within the promotional period.

Penalty APR Trigger

The Penalty APR of 39.99% will be applied to your account if: (1) you make a payment that is 60 or more days late; (2) a payment is returned for any reason. Once triggered, the Penalty APR may be applied to all existing balances and future transactions. We will review your account after 6 months of on-time minimum payments to determine whether to restore the standard APR. There is no guarantee of APR restoration.
